Passport update

Passport fees increased from 1 July 2026 for normal and Tatkaal applications.

If you are applying for a fresh passport, reissue, replacement, Tatkaal service, or overseas passport service, recheck the payable fee before booking the appointment or making payment.

Fees vary by service type, pages, age, normal or Tatkaal route, reissue reason, and overseas mission.

What changed?

Public reports based on the MEA fee revision say passport and related document service charges increased from 1 July 2026. Reported examples include a fresh or reissued ordinary 36-page adult passport rising from Rs. 1,500 to Rs. 2,500, and Tatkaal service charges rising to Rs. 5,000.

Who is affected?

  • Fresh adult passport applicants.
  • Applicants reissuing passports due to expiry, exhaustion of pages, loss, damage, or detail changes.
  • Tatkaal applicants.
  • Applicants for related services such as Police Clearance Certificate where applicable.
  • Indian citizens applying through missions or outsourcing partners abroad.

Practical steps before appointment

  • Use the official fee calculator before payment.
  • Confirm whether you need 36 pages or 60 pages.
  • Check whether normal processing is enough or Tatkaal is genuinely needed.
  • For lost or damaged passport reissue, verify the replacement category carefully.
  • For overseas applications, check the local embassy, consulate, or outsourcing partner fee schedule.
